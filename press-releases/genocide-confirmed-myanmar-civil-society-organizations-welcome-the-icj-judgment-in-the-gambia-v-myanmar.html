Genocide Confirmed: Myanmar Civil Society Organizations Welcome the ICJ Judgment in The Gambia v. Myanmar

November 19, 2026

Download PDF: English | Burmese

‍

We, the 219 undersigned Rohingya and other Myanmar civil society organizations, welcome and accept the final judgment issued today by the judges of the International Court of Justice in The Gambia v. Myanmar case concerning the application of the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide. The Court has found that Myanmar, through its military’s 2016 and 2017 “clearance operations” against the Rohingya, committed genocide, in breach of the Genocide Convention.

The highest court in the world has now confirmed what Rohingya survivors have told the world for nearly a decade, and what the United Nations Fact-Finding Mission found in 2018. The destruction of the Rohingya was not a tragedy or an excess of war. It was genocide, planned and carried out by the Myanmar military, and it can never again be denied.

We stand in solidarity with Rohingya victims and survivors. Not only do they live with the enduring trauma of those atrocities. For most of their community, they continue to live in exile and insecurity outside of their homeland, or face ongoing violence and persecution in Myanmar. Today’s judgment belongs to them, and to those who did not live to hear it.

The struggle for justice will continue, and this judgment must now be enforced. We call on the international community to do everything in your power to secure the full implementation of the judgment, including reparations and guarantees of non-repetition; the full recognition of their identity and restoration of their citizenship; the safe, voluntary, and dignified return of the Rohingya to their homeland; and to secure criminal accountability for Min Aung Hlaing and the leaders of the Myanmar military. They have destroyed our lives for too long.

Help us end their decades of barbarism against the Rohingya and all peoples of Myanmar. Help us free our country from their terror. Continue to stand with us. Continue to stand with the Rohingya.
